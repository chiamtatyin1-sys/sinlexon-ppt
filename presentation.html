Hardware Hardening

Engineered for Zero-Downtime Reliability

ECC Memory (Error-Correcting)

Standard across all tiers. Automatically detects and corrects single-bit memory corruptions. Critical for AI models that run 24/7 and crash on standard consumer RAM.

Enterprise PLP SSDs

Power-Loss Protection (PLP) via internal capacitor arrays. Prevents deep learning model and database block corruption if the office has an abrupt power cut.

ASPEED AST2600 BMC (IPMI)

Included in the RM100k Enterprise Tier. Out-of-band motherboard management. Hard reboot, configure BIOS, and check hardware sensors remotely even if the OS is frozen.

Titanium Power Efficiency

Seasonic/Corsair Titanium PSUs with 96%+ efficiency. Minimizes operating heat and lowers commercial utility bills by converting clean, stable power to high-draw GPUs.
