Data Privacy & PDPA Compliance

The Privacy Hazard of Cloud AI

⚠️ What happens when you use ChatGPT/Claude for business?

Every query is transmitted to and processed on US-based servers (OpenAI = San Francisco, Anthropic = San Francisco). This includes:

• Customer names, IC numbers, financial data
• Confidential legal contracts and M&A documents
• HR salary records and staff performance files
• Medical patient records (if used in clinics)

Malaysia's PDPA (Personal Data Protection Act 2010) Section 129 prohibits cross-border personal data transfer unless the destination country meets prescribed standards. The USA is not on Malaysia's approved list.
✅ Sinlexon Private Server = Zero Legal Risk

All data processing occurs on hardware physically located within your office or a Malaysian Tier-3 datacenter (AIMS/TM One, Cyberjaya). This means:

• Zero cross-border data transfer — 100% PDPA-compliant
• No data exposure to foreign intelligence agencies
• Fully auditable — complete data sovereignty

GPU Physical & Thermal Sizing

Why Card Size Matters for Server Builds

⚠️ RTX 5090 — Consumer Card Challenges

  • Massive triple-slot 3.5-slot cooler — requires enormous tower case, not rack-friendly
  • 600W TDP thermal design — requires large case airflow volume & additional exhaust fans
  • 16-pin 12V-2×6 connector — proprietary power delivery. Cable management difficult in 4U rack
  • Consumer-grade warranty — no enterprise SLA, limited downtime coverage

✅ RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell — Enterprise Design

  • Dual-slot blower design — compact, self-exhausting, rack-native from Day 1
  • 96GB unified VRAM — runs 230B+ parameter models impossible on any consumer card
  • 300W–600W configurable TDP — 54% less wall power than dual RTX 5090s under AI load
  • 3-year enterprise warranty — advance replacement SLA, certified for 24/7 datacenter use
The Conclusion: For serious enterprise AI workloads, one purpose-built RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell (RM55k) outperforms two consumer RTX 5090 cards (RM30k) in VRAM capacity, rack compatibility, power efficiency, and enterprise reliability.
