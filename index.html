AI Basics

What is AI? Explained simply.

No tech jargon. Just the basics you need to understand this investment.

WHAT IS A TOKEN?

The basic unit of AI text

A token is roughly 4 characters of text. "Hello world" = 2 tokens. 1,000 tokens ≈ 750 words (about 1.5 pages). AI models count tokens, not words. Pricing and limits are based on tokens.

Example: "Sinlexon AI Server Proposal" = 5 tokens. A typical email = 50-100 tokens. A 10-page document = 2,500-3,000 tokens.

WHAT IS AN LLM?

Large Language Model

An LLM is a neural network trained on massive amounts of text data. It predicts the next token in a sequence. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini — all are LLMs. The "large" means billions of parameters (math values learned during training).

Model sizes: 7B = 7 billion parameters (light). 70B = 70 billion (smart). 230B+ = 230 billion+ (very smart, needs lots of VRAM).

HOW DOES IT WORK?

Step by step

1. You type a question → 2. Text is split into tokens → 3. Tokens become numbers → 4. GPU does math (thousands of calculations) → 5. Numbers become tokens → 6. Tokens become text → 7. You get an answer.

Why GPU? A CPU has 16 cores. An RTX 5090 has 21,760 cores. AI needs thousands of simultaneous calculations — that's what GPUs are built for.

WHAT IS VRAM?

Video memory — the AI's workspace

VRAM stores the AI model. Bigger model = more VRAM needed. If the model doesn't fit in VRAM, it runs very slowly or not at all.

VRAM needed: 7B model ≈ 4GB. 14B model ≈ 8GB. 70B model ≈ 40GB. 230B model ≈ 96GB. RTX 5090 = 32GB. RTX PRO 6000 = 96GB.

Key Takeaway

AI is just math. Your GPU does the math. More GPU power = faster answers. More VRAM = smarter models. Local AI = your GPU, your VRAM, your data. Cloud AI = their GPU, their VRAM, their servers (and your data on their servers).

Why Air is Recommended for Servers

Noctua NH-D15 is an AIR cooler (not liquid). It's actually BETTER for servers because: no pump failure risk (AIO pumps die after 3-5 years), zero maintenance, same or better cooling than liquid. NH-D15 handles 250W+ easily (Ryzen 9950X = 170W TDP). Liquid cooling is optional for aesthetics only.

Storage Strategy: NVMe + HDD

1TB NVMe SSD (Samsung 990 Pro) — stores OS, AI models, and active data. Fast random access speed is critical for AI model loading. This stays in the server permanently.

SATA HDD bays — for bulk storage: company documents, ComfyUI outputs, video files, backups, RAG knowledge base. Speed doesn't matter much here. Customers buy their own HDDs when needed and just plug them in. No new server, no downtime.

Why not all SSD? SSDs are expensive per TB and have limited write endurance. HDDs are cheaper, more reliable for long-term archival, and perfect for bulk storage. AI models need NVMe speed — documents don't.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions answered

Everything you might ask.

Can it run 24/7?

Yes, but not recommended. Auto-shutdown at 6pm saves 60% electricity. If 24/7 is needed, add RM500-1,000/month for electricity depending on tier.

What if the GPU breaks?

All parts have 3-year warranty. ASUS/CMS Distribution will replace faulty components. Keep the original box and receipt.

Can we add more GPUs later?

RM30k: Can add 1 more GPU (becomes RM50k equivalent). RM50k: Can add more storage or a 2nd server. RM100k: Can add a 2nd PRO 6000 for double capacity.

Does it need internet?

Only for initial setup and model downloads. After that, it works completely offline. No internet = no data leak risk.

How loud is it?

Fractal Torrent and Lian Li O11 XL are among the quietest cases available. Under normal load: ~30-35dB (quieter than a conversation). Under heavy AI load: ~40-45dB (like a quiet office).

Can multiple people use it at once?

Yes. Open WebUI supports multiple concurrent users. RM30k: 2 light users. RM50k: 2-3 comfortable users. RM100k: 4+ heavy users or 10+ light users via API.

Can the admin see what users ask the AI?

Yes, the admin panel logs all queries. This is intentional — you want oversight of how your AI is being used. However, fine-tuning uses usage patterns only, not conversation content.

Is this better than ChatGPT?

For 90% of tasks: yes (same quality, free after break-even, private). For the top 10% of complex reasoning: ChatGPT-4o is still better. Hybrid approach recommended.
